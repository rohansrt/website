01 · Personal

Who am I?

I am a builder at heart. Over the last few years, I have built products, scaled teams, worked on future mobility platforms, and explored how technology can create meaningful impact at scale.

My journey has unfolded in four-year chapters. Four years preparing for competitive exams. Four years at IIT Roorkee learning engineering and problem solving. Four years at Jaguar Land Rover helping build India's Climate capability while working on electric mobility and sustainability. Most recently, I completed my MBA at ISB, where I broadened my perspective from engineering systems to business, strategy, and entrepreneurship.

What excites me most is ambition. I enjoy stepping into ambiguity, connecting ideas across disciplines, and building things that do not yet exist. Whether it is a product, a team, or a new venture, I am drawn to challenges that require both first-principles thinking and long-term vision.

Outside work, I am an avid chess player, a regular on the badminton and tennis court, and a lifelong anime enthusiast. Chess taught me to think several moves ahead. Racquet sports taught me resilience and adaptability. Anime nurtured my fascination with ambitious characters who challenge limits and pursue a purpose larger than themselves.

Having just completed ISB, I am looking to devote myself to something ambitious — something that combines technology, business, and human impact to solve meaningful problems at scale.
