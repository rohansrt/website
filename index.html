Who I am

My name is Rohan — a customer-centric, systems-oriented operator who has built teams, shipped platforms, designed AI products, and built early-stage ventures from scratch.

My life runs in 4-year leaps. Four years preparing for competitive exams. Four years at IIT Roorkee (GPA 8.4) building the engineering foundation. Four years at Jaguar Land Rover — as a seed member of India's Climate team, shipping the 2024 platform for 2L+ units across 100+ markets. Now at ISB, sharpening the business lens.
